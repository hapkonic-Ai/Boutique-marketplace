How it works

From first sketch to keepsake box

Every commission begins with a conversation. Tell us about the occasion, the colours your little one loves, a family motif — we'll take it from there. Most pieces take 2–3 weeks.

Begin a Commission
  1. I

    Consultation

    A relaxed chat at the studio, on a video call or over WhatsApp about the occasion, colours and little details.

  2. II

    Sketch & Swatches

    We hand-draw your design and courier fabric and thread swatches to your door for approval.

  3. III

    Stitching

    Our karigars smock, embroider and finish every seam by hand — with progress photos along the way.

  4. IV

    Keepsake Delivery

    Pressed, wrapped in tissue and shipped anywhere in India in a cotton keepsake box with a handwritten note.
