Our story

Born at the kitchen table

It began with a hand-me-down frock, a tin of embroidery thread and a little one who refused to wear anything else. Friends asked for one, then their friends did too.

Today our small studio still makes every piece the same way — by hand, in breathable mulmul and cotton made for Indian summers, in colours borrowed from the garden. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is made in thousands.
