Three generations of stitches

The techniques we use were taught at a grandmother's knee. We're keeping them alive, one little garment at a time.

The founder

“Every stitch is a small promise.”

Our founder learned smocking at her Nani's sewing table, watching rows of tiny pleats turn into rosebuds. Years later, making clothes for her own daughter, she couldn't find anything made with that same care — so she started making it herself.

House of Tarigai is still run from a small, sunlit studio where every piece is cut, smocked, embroidered and checked by hand before it reaches you.

— Founder's signature
